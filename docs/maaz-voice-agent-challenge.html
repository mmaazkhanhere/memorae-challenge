Must-haves

Six things that work for a real user.

Small and real beats big and faked. If something has to be mocked, say so.

1

Free onboarding in a minute

A link or a QR, /start, and it's working. Name and time zone asked once, or inferred. No forms, no card.

2

Reminders in plain words

Text or voice note. "In 5 minutes", "tomorrow at 9", "next Friday", "every Monday at 8". Right time zone, always. Ambiguous? It asks once.

3

Call it

A real voice call: phone number or WhatsApp call. It understands, confirms out loud with the exact time and channel, and handles being interrupted.

4

On time, exactly once

Reminders survive a restart or a deploy. Never late by more than a minute, never twice, never lost. "Done" closes it; "10 more minutes" snoozes it.

5

See, move, cancel

"What do I have tomorrow?", "move the bank one to 10", "cancel the gym". By text or by voice.

6

One memory, every channel

"Remember my gate code is 4512" on the call, "what's my gate code?" on Telegram. Same brain, wherever you talk to it.

Stretch

Pick what impresses. Only once the six work.

A

It calls you

For the reminders that matter, the agent phones you at the time instead of texting.

B

Remind someone else

"Remind Lina on Telegram tomorrow at 10 to sign." Only people who started the bot themselves.

C

Any language

Urdu, Spanish, English, mixed in one sentence. It answers in the language you spoke.

D

A tiny eval

50 reminder phrases, the time each should fire, and your parser's score. Show the misses.

E

Travel-proof

"I'm in Andorra now." Future reminders follow the new time zone; it tells you what moved.

Test script

What we'll try on day one.

Expect us to be messy, interrupt, and test edge cases. That's the point.

On the call

  1. "Remind me on Telegram in 5 minutes to send the contract."Confirms time and channel out loud. Arrives in 5 minutes.
  2. "Every Monday at 8, team standup."Recurring, in our time zone.
  3. "Remind me at 7 to call mum." (it's 15:00)Picks 19:00 or asks once. Never 07:00 tomorrow silently.
  4. "Actually, make it half an hour." (interrupting)Stops talking, updates the same reminder.
  5. "Remember the wifi password is Andorra2026."Saved to memory, not a reminder.

On Telegram

  1. "What's the wifi password?"Andorra2026, from the call.
  2. "What do I have tomorrow?"A short list, in our time.
  3. "Cancel the standup."Cancels the series, confirms which one.
  4. Voice note: "remind me Friday to renew the domain"No hour given: proposes one, we confirm.
  5. You restart the server.Nothing lost, nothing sent twice.

Ground rules

Simple ones.

  • Solo. AI coding tools are welcome. Tell us where they helped and where you overruled them.
  • Any stack, any LLM, any voice provider. LangGraph is fine if it earns its place.
  • Free tiers and trial credits should cover it; keep spend small.
  • Only your own data and people who said yes. Store as little as you can.
  • Stuck more than two hours on an approval (WhatsApp, a phone number)? Use the next best option, write it in the README, keep going.
  • It's a test, not client work: the code stays yours.
